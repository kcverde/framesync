FrameSync includes Qt shared libraries, the PySide6 Python bindings, and Shiboken
from The Qt Company and Qt contributors. They are distributed separately inside
Contents/Frameworks. The Qt and Qt for Python open-source license terms are
available in the accompanying LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt files.

Upstream sources:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
The exact PySide6 and Shiboken versions are recorded in the project’s uv.lock.
